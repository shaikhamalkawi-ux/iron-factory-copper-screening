## Relationship to the article and data

The thesis is primary **historical source documentation** for the site description, soil/ash collection, operational chemical extraction steps, FAAS, and reported QA/QC. It does **not** establish independent calibration or hardware configuration for the later linked XLF magnetic readings. It contains historical source discrepancies, which the 2026 manuscript discusses rather than silently correcting. The 2026 paper's fixed-budget screening analysis is a separate secondary analysis, not a reproduction of a previous thesis result.

The public sanitized numeric workbook and reproducibility scripts are in this repository; the original/private workbook and the reviewer correspondence are **not** published. The earlier Zenodo DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23019081](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23019081) is for an independent dataset/software deposit and is not a thesis DOI.
